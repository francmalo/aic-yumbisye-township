Our Story

Rooted in Faith,
Growing Together

AIC Yumbisye Township is a Spirit-led congregation committed to the transformative power of the Gospel — part of Africa Inland Church Kenya, one of Africa's most respected evangelical denominations.

Since our founding we have been a beacon of hope, a centre of spiritual growth, and a family that walks together through every season of life.
